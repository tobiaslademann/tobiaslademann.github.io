Hello! I am a research associate at [PTW](https://www.ptw.tu-darmstadt.de/) at the Technical University of Darmstadt. In my research, I focus on controlling industrial energy supply systems using reinforcement learning. My goal is to explore how RL can be reliably applied in real-world systems to reduce energy consumption and costs.

More specifically, I am interested in the following research areas:

- Dynamic simulation of (thermal) energy systems (Modelica, Dymola)
- Real-World Reinforcement Learning
- Industrial building automation systems
